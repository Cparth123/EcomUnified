## 🔵 PROMPT 1: Master 23-Dimension Sourcing & Stress-Test Dossier
**Use Case:** Comprehensive 23-section institutional-grade financial analysis covering exact Indian eCommerce unit economics, reverse logistics classification, and multi-tier sensitivity testing.

### Key Analysis Dimensions:
1. **Product Information Extraction & AI Vision:**
   - **Product Title & Brand:** High-converting marketplace title and OEM/Brand model.
   - **Detected Category & Sub-Category:** Exact product classification (e.g., Home & Kitchen > Storage > Lunch Bags).
   - **Official HSN Code:** Indian 4-digit / 6-digit Harmonized System Nomenclature (e.g., `HSN 4202.92`, `HSN 8518.30`, `HSN 9404.90`).
   - **Applicable GST Tax Rate:** Exact GST slab (`5%`, `12%`, `18%`, or `28%`).
   - **Recommended Marketplace Listing Category Node:** Optimal fee bracket category for Amazon India and Flipkart.
   - **Physical Specs:** Weight in grams, dimensions (L×W×H cm), material, and pack type.
2. **Return Policy & Reverse Logistics Classification:** Category return policy window (7/10 days), replacement vs refund, and resalability rating (Resalable vs Hygiene write-off).
3. **6-Month Market Demand Trajectory:** Search volume trends, sales velocity, seasonality, and saturation index.
4. **Competitor Listing Benchmarks:** Top 4 matching live listings on Amazon India and Flipkart with ratings, prices, and review counts.
5. **Product Landed Cost Calculation:** Supplier buying price + inbound freight + packaging materials + GST on goods.
6. **Amazon & Flipkart Fee Engines:** Referral/commission fee, fixed closing fee, shipping & weight handling fee, collection fee, and 18% GST on platform fees.
7. **Net In-Hand Bank Payout & Unit Margin:** Exact bank payout, net profit per unit, and net margin percentage.
8. **Selling Price Scenarios & Break-Even:** Low, balanced, and high pricing matrices with break-even calculations.
9. **Return & RTO Sensitivity Analysis:** Stress-tested net profit at 5%, 10%, 20%, 30%, and 50% combined return/RTO rates.
10. **TACoS & Advertising Tolerance:** Maximum allowable ad spend before slipping into per-unit losses.
11. **Working Capital & Reserve Allocation:** Recommended safety stock and cash reserves for COD returns.
12. **Final Sourcing Verdict & Executive Summary:** Clear BUY / BUY WITH CAUTION / DON'T BUY decision with actionable reasoning.

## 🟢 PROMPT 2: Multi-Platform 7-Step Profitability & Sales Volume Matrix
**Use Case:** Rapid 7-step analysis evaluating wholesale products (from Telegram/Surat catalog screenshots) across Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, JioMart, and IndiaMart with volume profit projections.

### Step 1: AI Vision Product Identification, Category & HSN Code Extraction
- **Product Title & Brand/OEM:** High-converting commercial title identified from image.
- **Detected Category & Sub-Category:** Primary product taxonomy.
- **Official HSN Code:** Indian GST HSN code (e.g., `HSN 4202.92` for insulated lunch bags, `HSN 8518` for neckbands, `HSN 9404` for pillows).
- **Applicable GST Tax Rate:** `5%`, `12%`, `18%`, or `28%` GST rate.
- **Recommended Marketplace Listing Category Node:** Best category to list under on Amazon & Flipkart for lowest referral fee & max visibility.
- **Wholesale Sourcing Price & MOQ:** Supplier rate (₹) and minimum order quantity.
- **Category Photo Matching:** Category-specific photo verification style.

### Step 2: Existing Listings & Multi-Platform Check
- Search and compare listings across Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, and JioMart.
- Compare selling price, MRP, discounts, rating stars, and seller fulfillment model.

### Step 3: Platform Fee Deductions & Landed Cost
- Sourcing Landed Cost = Supplier Buying Price + Freight + Packaging + GST.
- Compare platform deductions: Amazon (Referral + Closing + Easy Ship) vs Flipkart (Commission + Fixed + Shipping).

### Step 4: Real In-Hand Bank Payout & Unit Margin
- Calculate net bank deposit and profit per unit for each platform.
- Calculate exact break-even minimum selling price.

### Step 5: Return Policy & Courier RTO Sensitivity
- Classify return window and customer return vs courier RTO impact.
- Evaluate resalability (inspect & repack vs unsellable scrap loss).
